## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory intimacy of high-couture European bridal editorial and heritage bespoke stationery. It fuses classic tactile materiality—cotton deckle-edged paper, warm melted wax seals, fine-gauge metallic hot stamping—with modern, whisper-quiet digital choreography. 

The emotional arc is deliberate, poetic, and reverent: an invitation experience designed to feel like unwrapping an heirloom linen envelope rather than navigating an ephemeral web page. The visual language rejects standard web conventions in favor of generous margins, delicate ornamental framing, and nuanced tonal contrasts that celebrate romantic ceremony.

### Design Movement & Ethos
- **Tactile Editorial Romance:** Subtle cotton textures, layered translucent cards, and warm metallic accents paired with classical serif proportions.
- **European Monastic Minimalism:** Generous vertical pacing, breathing room between ceremonial milestones, and disciplined typographic hierarchy.
- **Organic Botanicals:** Warm mementos of desaturated eucalyptus foliage, delicate hand-rendered linework, and soft gilded rings.

## Colors

The palette balances warm earthen anchors with luminescent botanical and metallic highlights, evoking ivory linen, champagne silk, and weathered Tuscan stone.

### Color Roles & Atmosphere
- **Primary (`#8C6D58` - Warm Moka Taupe):** The foundational voice of the narrative. Used for primary interactive actions, formal monograms, key event milestones, and grounding block transitions. An intimate darker variant (`#6E5343`) anchors deep ceremonial panels and high-contrast typography.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880` - Antique Leaf Gold):** The touch of gilded foil. Applied to delicate concentric rings, double hairline borders, hairline rules, ornamental filigree, and active focus cues.
- **Tertiary (`#7A8B7B` - Desaturated Eucalyptus):** Botanical balance. Used for botanical illustrations, status indicators, serene accents, and RSVP acceptance tags.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5` - Soft Handmade Ivory):** The primary canvas. Mimics 300gsm deckle-edge cotton rag. Complemented by `#F5EFEB` (Champagne Cream) for subtle layered inner cards and contextual panels.
- **Ink Typography (`#3B322C` - Deep Espresso Umber):** Softened from harsh pure black to preserve the gentle intimacy of printed letterpress.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a classical dialogue between high-contrast editorial serifs and contemporary geometric clarity.

### Typographic Roles
- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the grandeur of Italian wedding programs. It is calibrated with generous line-heights and slight letter-tracking to let each character breathe.
- **Modern Clarity Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for high legibility on mobile screens for crucial logistical data (schedules, Google Maps locations, dress codes, banking details).
- **Ceremonial Metadata (`Montserrat` in All-Caps):** Used strictly with ultra-wide letter spacing (`0.15em` to `0.25em`) for dates, status chips, section headers, and dress code annotations.
- **Calligraphic Accents:** Where digital script flourishes (e.g. initials or couple monograms) appear, they function as standalone graphical focal elements, flanked by generous empty space.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system adopts a centered, editorial portrait canvas reminiscent of physical luxury cards. Layout containers are bounded to a maximum width of `480px` on mobile and `680px` on tablet/desktop displays to recreate the authentic aspect ratio of a stationery suite.

### Layout Principles
- **Atmospheric Pacing:** Large vertical separations (`space-xl` and higher) are intentionally placed between liturgical sections (Ceremony, Reception, Itinerary, Gallery) to give each event chapter singular focus.
- **Concentric Margins:** Outer sections provide generous breathing space (`margin`), framing inner parchment layers with dual-line insets.
- **Mobile First Adaptation:** On viewport widths under `640px`, layout gutters contract to `1rem`, allowing imagery and gilded borders maximum edge exposure while keeping text comfortably within 20px padding zones.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use cold drop shadows or standard digital elevation tiers. Instead, depth is achieved through tactile stationery layering and warm ambient illumination.

### Materiality & Stacking
- **Parchment Base (Level 0):** Soft ivory background (`#FBF9F5`) with micro-grain noise (representing 100% cotton fiber).
- **Pressed Invitation Card (Level 1):** Champagne Cream (`#F5EFEB`) surfaces bounded by double hairline borders (`1px solid #C5A88040` exterior, `1px solid #C5A88020` interior inset by 4px).
- **Ambient Shadow Crafting:** When floating cards or Polaroid-style gallery captures hover, they project an ultra-diffused, warm umber drop shadow: `0 14px 34px -8px rgba(110, 83, 67, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(110, 83, 67, 0.06)`.
- **Melted Wax Seal (Level 2):** Cast in deep moka taupe (`#6E5343`) with an internal bevel highlight and soft localized perimeter shadow: `0 6px 16px rgba(60, 42, 30, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The form language balances clean architectural stationery with soft, welcoming radii. 

### Corner Geometry
- **Outer Stationery Cards:** Use moderate `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`) corners, paying homage to smooth, hand-cut deckle borders.
- **Pill Elements:** Interactive action buttons, wax stamp bases, and countdown capsules use full-radius pill silhouettes (`9999px`) to create a smooth, touch-friendly tactile feel.
- **Concentric Golden Arcs:** Linear wireframes, floral crests, and date wreaths retain pure circular geometry.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Confirmar Asistencia", "Ingresar a mi invitación"):** Full pill shape, filled with `#8C6D58` (Moka Taupe), displaying centered `#FBF9F5` typography in `label-caps` or medium serif. Subtle hover/active transforms include a warm gilded glow (`0 0 0 1px #C5A880`) and a slight scale transition (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Action (e.g., "Ver Mapa", "Agendar en Calendario"):** Transparent surface encased in a 1px antique gold hairline border (`#C5A880`), text styled in `#6E5343` uppercase tracking.

### Countdown Timers & Numeric Displays
- Individual time blocks are displayed in clean column pairs. Figures use `date-display` in `#8C6D58`, underlined by `label-caps` in `#C5A880` (DÍAS, HORAS, MIN, SEG). Dividers use subtle vertical gold hairlines.

### Timeline & Itinerary Nodes
- A central vertical path rendered in `#C5A88080` (0.75px width).
- Milestone icons (Church, Rings, Cocktail, Banquet, Dance) sit inside circular ivory capsules accented with a fine gold ring.
- Times are rendered in bold serif numerals alongside understated labels.

### Gift Registry & Banking Panels
- Contained within dedicated warm moka containers (`#8C6D58`) with crisp ivory typography, or creamy cards with double gold borders.
- Copyable account numbers (IBAN/CLABE) include an integrated tactile "Copiar" button with instantaneous micro-feedback ("Copiado").

### Interactive RSVP Form
- **Inputs:** Minimalist hairline underline inputs on ivory background (`border-bottom: 1px solid #C5A880; background: transparent;`). Text uses `body-md` in `#3B322C`. Focus transitions deepen the bottom line to `#8C6D58`.
- **Radio & Acceptance Selectors:** Bespoke circular selectors adorned with delicate check marks colored in eucalyptus green (`#7A8B7B`).
